  • Built a RAG evaluation harness before touching retrieval — 40 labelled questions over 120 notes, recall@k and MRR@k, significance by paired bootstrap at 10,000 resamples; production baseline recall@5 0.963, MRR@10 0.886. 54 tests across chunking, metrics, rerankers and integration.
