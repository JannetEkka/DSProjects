The Company

JTech Solutions (pre-incorporation) — Founder · SMT is the first product
Jan 2024 – Present · Kolkata
  • What it is. Written rules with years of tested history open every trade. AI advisors explain each decision and are graded on whether they were right. Before a reason reaches a user, the system flips that advisor's input and re-runs the decision; if nothing changes, the reason is never shown.
  • How it earns. Exchanges share trading-fee revenue with whoever brings and keeps users. In SMT World an exchange's users sign in with their exchange account, watch the agent trade in a world they explore, and see why. We earn through the affiliate programmes exchanges already run.
  • Stage. The agent has placed real orders 24/7 since January on WEEX's simulated account (about $10K). SMT World is live. No revenue yet.
  • Traction. WEEX AI Wars I: first on the preliminary leaderboard of 230+ teams, then the 37-team finals, and a WEEX feature on SMT. WEEX gave SMT a $10K simulated account, assigned an affiliate manager, and shipped an API endpoint after our feedback. A partnerships contact at Bitunix approached me. At a July builder night, students new to crypto tried SMT World and about 10 joined as early users or contributors.
  • Evidence. 1,800 strategy variants, 36 million simulated trades from 2020 to 2026, priced at six exchanges' published fees. Nothing holding a position under four hours cleared its fees. The crash-buy rule that did: 312 trades, 73% won, +4.76% average after fees, positive in every year. A register of 611 measured findings decides what ships.
  • What changed my mind. My bot topped the prelims mostly by shorting a falling market with high leverage, then lost the finals. Replayed on six years of data, those rules lost money in almost every year, and my six AI agents could not predict direction. So I flipped the design: rules with long records open trades, and the AI explains and gets graded.
  • IP. Provisional patent, India, App. No. 202631090789, filed myself in July 2026: the AI can veto a trade but never cause one, every explanation is checked by counterfactual test, and the system retunes itself only through statistical gates. Complete specification due July 2027.
  • Built solo. 91,000 lines of Python, 3,000+ tests and 344 merged pull requests, written by AI coding agents I direct and reviewed by me before every merge. Moved the live system to a zero-cost stack (Oracle Cloud, Cloudflare Workers, Google AI Studio) in two days. All of it while a full-time family carer.
  • Next 12 months. Six months of live record on the two live rules; paper rules promoted after 30 tracked trades each; SMT World live with a first exchange; the full patent specification filed.
